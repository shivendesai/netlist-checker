documentation

Design rule reference

Every check Netlist Checker runs, what it looks for, and what triggers a failure.

parse_integrityError

Reference Integrity

Verifies that every pin reference inside a net actually corresponds to a component defined in the netlist. A mismatch means the schematic has a dangling or mis-typed reference designator.

Fails when: a net lists a pin on component R99 but R99 does not appear in the components section. Common cause: renaming a component in the schematic without updating net annotations.
power_net_presenceError

Power & Ground Net Presence

Checks that the design contains at least one recognised power net (VCC, 3V3, 5V, VBAT, VDD) and at least one ground net (GND, AGND, DGND, PGND).

Fails when: neither a power nor a ground net can be found. Common cause: power symbols were not added to the schematic, or the netlist was exported before the power section was connected.
net_stubWarning

Net Stub Detection

Flags any net with fewer than two connections. A net with only one pin connected is a stub — the signal is driven but never received (or vice versa), which is almost always unintentional.

Fails when: a net has exactly one pin. Common cause: a wire was started but not connected to the destination component, or a component was deleted without removing its net label.
spi_bus_completenessWarning

SPI Bus Completeness

For each SPI bus found in the design (SPI1, SPI2, SPI3), confirms that all three required signal nets are present: SCK, MOSI, and MISO.

Fails when: a bus is partially wired — e.g. SPI1_SCK and SPI1_MOSI exist but SPI1_MISO is missing. Common cause: read-only peripheral assumed MISO was not needed, or a net was accidentally left unnamed.
spi_pin_assignmentError

SPI Pin Assignment (STM32F103)

Cross-references SPI1 nets against the STM32F103 CubeMX pin map: SPI1_SCK → PA5, SPI1_MOSI → PA7, SPI1_MISO → PA6. Catches swapped lines before layout.

Fails when: SPI1_MOSI is routed to U1 pin PA6 instead of PA7 (a common MISO/MOSI swap). Common cause: copy-pasting pin assignments from a datasheet without verifying against the CubeMX configuration.
decoupling_capacitorWarning

Decoupling Capacitor Presence

For each IC (U*, IC*) connected to a recognised power net, verifies that at least one capacitor (C*) is also on that same power net.

Fails when: U3 is powered by 3V3 but no capacitor shares that net. Common cause: decoupling caps were placed in the layout but never tied to the schematic net, or were simply forgotten.
footprint_consistencyInfo

Footprint Family Consistency

Checks that passive components use footprints from the expected SMD library family: resistors (R*) should use Resistor_SMD, capacitors (C*) Capacitor_SMD, and inductors (L*) Inductor_SMD.

Flags when: R5 has a footprint from Resistor_THT while the rest of the board is SMD. Informational — through-hole passives are sometimes intentional — but worth a review before sending to fab.
gnd_continuityError

Ground Net Continuity

Verifies that every IC with an identifiable ground pin (GND, AGND, DGND, PGND) is actually connected to a ground net in the netlist. An IC with a floating ground pin will not function on the board.

Fails when: a component reference appears in the schematic but its GND pin has no net connection. Common cause: a ground symbol was placed near a pin visually but not electrically connected, or a multi-unit component has an unplaced power unit.
